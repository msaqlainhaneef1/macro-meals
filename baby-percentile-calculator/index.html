Understanding Growth Percentiles

WHO growth charts track how babies grow compared to other healthy babies worldwide. Consistent growth along a curve matters more than the specific percentile number.

Frequently Asked Questions

If your baby is at the 50th percentile for weight, it means they weigh more than 50% of babies their age. Any percentile between 5th and 95th is generally considered normal.
